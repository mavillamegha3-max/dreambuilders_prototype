ABOUT OUR PROJECT

Introducing AgroJal

A smart water management concept designed to make irrigation control simpler, more convenient, and more accessible for farmers.

🌱

🌾 Our Vision

Our vision is to support smarter water management by combining irrigation equipment with easy-to-use digital technology. AgroJal aims to help users manage water flow conveniently and make more informed decisions about water use.

🎯 The Problem We Want to Solve

Traditional water management may require users to operate pumps and valves manually or travel to the equipment to change settings. This can take time and make water management less convenient.

Users may also find it difficult to keep track of water consumption without appropriate monitoring equipment.

AgroJal explores how connected controls, flow monitoring, and a simple dashboard could help address these challenges.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Does AgroJal control a real water pump?

No. The current version is a website prototype. The pump button only changes the displayed demonstration status. Physical control requires compatible hardware, a suitable driver or relay, a communication method, and safety measures.

Are the water-usage values real?

No. The displayed readings and chart use sample data. Real measurements would require a compatible flow sensor, a controller, and software to process and transmit the readings.

Why does the flow-rate slider not change the actual water flow?

The slider currently updates only the number displayed on the website. A real system would need suitable flow-control hardware, such as a compatible motorized valve or pump controller, along with appropriate feedback and safety checks.

Does the website support multiple languages?

The prototype includes English, Telugu, Hindi, and Tamil options. Supported navigation labels and greetings change with the selection. Not all page content has been translated yet.

Why is the chart not appearing?

The chart library loads from an external service. Check your internet connection and reload the website. The rest of the website can still display without the chart library.

Is the login connected to a database?

No. Login is for demonstration only. The website does not currently authenticate users against a database. A real application would require secure authentication and appropriate protection for user information.

Can I use AgroJal on a mobile phone?

Yes. The website layout is responsive and is designed to adapt to smaller screens. Some features, such as charts, may appear differently depending on the device.

🛠️ Troubleshooting

Problem: The Continue button does not work.

Check that you have entered a name and a password containing at least four characters.

Problem: The language does not change.

Return to Settings, select the required language, and click Save Language Preference.

Problem: The pump status does not represent the physical pump.

This is expected in demonstration mode. The current website has no physical hardware connection.

Problem: The website design looks incorrect.

Confirm that index.html, style.css, and script.js are saved in the same folder. Refresh the browser after saving your changes.
